🏰 HOW TO PLAY TOWERS

🕊️ 5-Minute Truce

• You start with a 5-Minute Truce (05:00). Enemies will not raid during this time.

• Use this time to mine gold in the basement, construct floors, and recruit your army!

• Attacking enemy fortresses will break the truce early.

🎮 Direct Control & Movement

• Click any stick figure to instantly take direct control of them!

• Use A / D to run left and right.

• Use W / S to climb ladders connecting the basement and all tower floors.

• Press Space or Left Click to swing your weapon or mine the gold vault in the basement.

• Press ESC to release direct control.

💰 Basement Treasury & Tower Floors

• The Basement Vault generates money! Order stick figures down there to work, or manually strike the gold vein yourself.

• Buy new floors for your tower to unlock archer balconies, armory stat boosts, and roof ballistas.

• Stick figures can traverse all floors and ladders seamlessly.

⚔️ AI Conquest & War

• West AI (Red Vanguard) and East AI (Shadow Legion) will periodically raid your citadel!

• Order your stick figures to assault their fortresses, breach their gates, and destroy their cores to conquer them.
